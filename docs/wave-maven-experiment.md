# Wave-Maven image experiment

This is an exploratory research baseline, **not a diagnostic model** and not connected to Cardioscopio's inference path. It uses the Wave-Maven reference case list and one ECG PDF per case, cached locally under the ignored `artifacts/` directory. The reference index and answers were reviewed before the experiment, so even its held-out case split is not an independent external validation set. No evidence here establishes a diagnostic improvement or supports clinical use.

Run from the repository root after completing the companion case review and PDF download:

```bash
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python training/wave-maven-experiment/run.py
```

The environment needs Python, NumPy, SciPy, Pillow, scikit-learn and Poppler's `pdftoppm` and `pdftotext` commands. Exact versions are written to `artifacts/wave-maven-experiment/results.json`, together with the seed (20261113), sample accounting, split sizes and per-category results. `excluded.json` records excluded cases and reasons; `splits.json` records the case allocation. Outputs and the original ECGs remain ignored and are not distributed by this experiment. Reproduction requires authorized access to the Wave-Maven materials; a user assertion of permission is not documentation of a license or redistribution rights.

The script uses image pixels from a cropped ECG sheet, removes extractable PDF text bounding boxes, masks typical locations for lead text and calibration pulses, suppresses the red grid and downsamples the remaining ink. It does not supply case IDs, filenames, answer text, history, or diagnosis strings as model features. PDF text masking and fixed-position masking cannot guarantee removal of text or identifiers already rasterized into an image; changes in page layout can also make the crop miss parts of the trace. Labels come from the companion catalogue's keyword-derived, nonexclusive categories. Unlisted categories are treated as negatives even though the catalogue is not exhaustive; this creates label noise and does not establish clinical absence. Unmapped cases and missing, invalid, or unreadable assets are excluded and counted.

Before any model fitting, cases are grouped by identical PDF SHA-256 or close image perceptual hash (Hamming distance at most four), then groups are randomly assigned 60/20/20 to training, model selection and held-out test. This grouping is heuristic and cannot rule out all near duplicates. Patient IDs are unavailable, so the partitions cannot be shown to be patient-independent. For categories with at least 12 training positives and three selection positives, a class-balanced, regularized logistic classifier is selected using average precision on the selection split. A fixed 0.5 score threshold is used for the descriptive precision, recall and case-level abstention counts; these scores are not calibrated probabilities. Reported Wilson intervals are unadjusted for patient clustering, label noise and multiple comparisons. Small class counts and the broad, nonexclusive labels make the scores highly uncertain. Neither selection nor test results have been validated prospectively or independently.

In the completed run, all 547 PDFs were available and yielded mapped, renderable inputs: 328 cases were assigned to training, 110 to selection and 109 to test. These are case counts, not patient counts. The heuristic formed 414 groups, including one connected group of 126 cases; this unusually large group may reflect false similarity links and further limits the split's representativeness. Ten categories met the training and selection support rules. Seven did not, including ventricular arrhythmia/ectopy; they have no fitted classifier. Some fitted categories have as few as two positive test cases. All 109 test cases received at least one category score of 0.5 or more, so the fixed-threshold case-level abstention rate was zero. This does not establish confidence or correctness. See the local `results.json` for per-category counts and estimates rather than interpreting an aggregate score as a diagnosis rate. Experimental fitted coefficients and the scaler are saved only under ignored `artifacts/wave-maven-experiment/experimental-weights.npz` for inspection and are not used by the application.

The reviewable [results](../training/wave-maven-experiment/results.json) and [case split manifest](../training/wave-maven-experiment/splits.json) contain no source images, extracted features, clinical histories or diagnosis-answer text. IDs in the manifest identify the assigned cases; they are not model inputs. Only the 328 training cases adjusted classifier weights. The other 219 cases were used for selection and testing, respectively, and were not used for fitting the final selected classifier. All 547 cases were processed, but all 547 were not used to fit weights. The estimated performance is low and uneven across categories, and the weights remain inactive. To recreate and inspect the weights locally, obtain the PDFs through the companion review workflow, run the command above, and open `artifacts/wave-maven-experiment/experimental-weights.npz` with NumPy. A second run with the cached features produced byte-identical `results.json` in this environment; it is a repeatability check, not independent validation.
