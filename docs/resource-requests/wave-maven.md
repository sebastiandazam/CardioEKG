CardioEKG is free to use and is being developed as an open-source project. Its guided workflow places the ECG in clinical context, supports local reconstruction of authorized ECG images, presents measurements and observations for each lead, and relates the tracing to reference cardiac anatomy and schematic electrical conduction. We are also developing a downloadable educational report with measurement provenance, confidence, limitations and clinical references.

The prototype is experimental. Its current reconstruction and measurement methods are not independently clinically validated, and no diagnostic model should be understood as an approved clinical device. We are improving and evaluating separate methods for waveform delineation, rate, intervals, frontal QRS axis and diagnostic patterns. Default image processing is local in the browser. Any future remote inference would be optional and require explicit consent. The public edition is for simulated ECGs or public examples whose use is authorized; a private installation for patient records would require separate institutional validation and authorization.

Wave-Maven's expertly explained cases would be especially valuable for educational review and independent evaluation. I understand that public access and a known diagnosis do not automatically grant permission for machine-learning training or redistribution. We have not treated the website as an open training dataset.

Could you advise whether you would consider a no-cost educational permission or collaboration covering any of the following, with each permission distinguished explicitly?

1. Use of selected de-identified ECG images and accompanying case diagnoses for model training or fine-tuning.
2. Use of a separate, held-out set for evaluation, without including those cases in training or model selection.
3. Display of selected images, case explanations or annotations within a free student-facing application and downloadable reports, with the attribution you require.
4. Access to available waveform samples, calibration, verified intervals or P/QRS/T boundary annotations, if these can be shared. A case diagnosis alone would not be treated as expert annotation of waveform boundaries.
5. Publication of aggregate evaluation results and redistribution of trained model weights or other permitted derived artifacts in an open-source educational project, separately from redistribution of the original case material.
